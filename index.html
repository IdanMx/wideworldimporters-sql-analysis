Only a handful of places actually need reserving — most great food in Japan is walk-in or a queue. These are the ones worth locking in now, roughly in order of urgency. Book on the restaurant's site or TableCheck / Tabelog; your hotel front desk can also phone for you.

Restaurants

Matsusakagyu Yakiniku M — Hozenji Yokocho, Osaka Book ~1 week ahead Grill-your-own Matsusaka wagyu — the one big beef splurge of the trip, and slots for 4 go fast in August. Book via TableCheck. Aim for an Osaka evening (Aug 6–10).
Kani Doraku Honten — Dotonbori, Osaka Book 3–5 days ahead The famous moving-crab sign — full crab dinner, kid-friendly, tables with a Dotonbori view. Reserve on the official site (English available).
bills — Odaiba, Tokyo Book 2–3 days ahead Ricotta pancakes with a bay view — the easy sit-down anchor for the Odaiba day (Block 2). Reserve on the official site.
Tonkatsu Maisen — Aoyama, Tokyo Book 2–3 days ahead Famous tonkatsu in a converted 1930s bathhouse, 10 min from Harajuku (Block 3). Reservable via Tabelog/TableCheck — or walk in off-peak (~14:30).
Monja Kondō Honten — Tsukishima, Tokyo Book 1–2 days ahead (or queue) Cook-it-yourself monjayaki one metro stop from the hotel — perfect first-night dinner. Ask the hotel desk to phone (+81 3-3533-4555), or just queue on Monja Street and take any shop with a line.
Kura Sushi — Ginza, Tokyo Same day, via app Conveyor-belt sushi with the prize-capsule game (Bikkura-Pon) — grab a queue ticket on the Kura Sushi app an hour before you go.

Attractions with fixed slots (from Mom's plan)

Universal Studios Japan — Super Nintendo World Book now Buy park tickets + Express Pass with Nintendo-area entry for an August date — they sell out. Kinopio's Cafe (inside) doesn't take bookings; go at an off-hour. Free timed-entry to the Nintendo area is also drawn in the USJ app the morning of.
teamLab Borderless — Azabudai Hills Already planned — confirm slot Timed tickets; afterwards eat downstairs at Azabudai Hills Market.
Tokyo Disney / DisneySea Already planned — confirm date Buy dated tickets in the official app; Premier Access for big rides opens in-app on the day.
Shibuya Sky Book 1–2 weeks ahead for sunset Sunset slots (~18:00–18:40 in August) sell out first — book online for the Block 3 day; any clear evening works.
